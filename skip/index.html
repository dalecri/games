SKIP

You're a magic skipping stone, flying over a world that's mostly water.

Tapjust before you touch a surface: bounce and speed up. Nail the timing for a Perfect. Holdto fly flatter and skim across. It's low, fast and safe on open water. Let goof a hold just before you land: a big high pop over rocks and rooftops.

Do nothing when you hit water and you plop, losing a lot of speed. Too slow and you sink. Hitting the side of a rock or building shatters you.
