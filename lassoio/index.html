Lasso.io

You drag a rope behind you. Ride a full circle so your path crosses your own rope, and anyone caught inside the loop is lassoed. Every catch makes your rope longer.

Loops also scoop up the gold inside them. An empty loop frays your rope a little. Ride across someone's rope to cut it short. A long rope makes big loops, but it's a big target too.
