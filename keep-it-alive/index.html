Keep it alive

This little thing moves on its own. You can't steer it. You get one object in the water at a time, and placing a new one removes the old one.

Food, tap: it swims over to eat it Wall, drag: blocks everything Bumper, tap: flings whatever touches it Portal, drag from one end to the other: anything entering comes out the other side

The longer it lives, the stranger the water gets.
