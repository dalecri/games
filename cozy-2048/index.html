How to play

    
Slide and merge
Swipe, or use the arrow keys. Blooms slide as far as they can, and two of a kind grow into the next one.

    
Play until you are stuck
The run ends when no move is left. Your harvest is the five highest blooms on the board, added up. The basket shows them as you go.

    
Watch the seasons
The season turns every 20 moves and bends one rule.

    
Watch the garden grow
The island starts each run bare. Every bloom you make takes root there, and visitors turn up as you climb higher. Tap it for a closer look.
