Tether

Hold → tether → swing → let go → fly

Press and hold near a rock, balloon or drone to throw your rope at it. If you press in open sky, it grabs the best anchor within reach. Let go to release.

Release while you're moving up and forward for a Perfect: a speed boost and a longer chain. Bump into rocks or towers and you lose your momentum. The storm behind you never stops, and the storm sea below is the end.
