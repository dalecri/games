Glide

dive → speed up → pull up → soar → slow down → dive

You're a paper airplane with no engine. Hold to pull the nose up and climb, which costs speed. Let go and the nose drops into a dive, which earns the speed back.

Climb too slowly and you stall. The air drains a little energy all the time, so ride the rising air (shimmering columns) and tailwinds to stay up. Cities, caves and storms arrive the further you fly.
