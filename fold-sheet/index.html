FOLD

Drag from any edge of the sheet toward the middle to fold it over. The crease lands halfway between the edge and your finger.

Two dots of the same colour that land on each other pop. Different colours can't touch, so that fold won't go through. Ink blots don't need clearing, but nothing else can land on them. Pinned squares can't be folded. Holes swallow any dot that lands on them.

Pop every dot in as few folds as you can.
